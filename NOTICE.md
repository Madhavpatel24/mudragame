# Notices

## Mudra photographs

The mudra photographs in `game/public/mudras/` come from the **Bharatanatyam Mudra Dataset**
(Jisha Raj R and colleagues, College of Engineering, Attingal, Kerala), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The photographs were cropped, had their green studio background removed and replaced with black, and
were resized. Under the licence, these adapted images are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.

## Samyukta model and code

`vendor/samyukta-hasta-game/` is Aparna's Samyukta Hasta game and model, included unchanged. The meanings
of the Samyukta mudras in the game come from its teacher reference
(`outputs/teacher/teacher_reference.json`).

## Asamyukta meanings

The Asamyukta meanings in `game/lib/content/meanings.ts` are a draft written from the standard descriptions
of the single-hand gestures. A dance teacher should check them before release.
